## 4. RQ5 — selection bias under repeated stratified splits

The 200 selection samples (26 positive) are split 1000 times into two stratified halves; the best of the 8 prompts (3 manual, 5 generated) on one half is evaluated on the other.

- optimism of the winner (F1 on its selection half minus F1 on the other half): mean 0.027, 95% interval [-0.143, 0.247]
- the winner is also the best prompt on the other half in 57% of splits
- a generated prompt wins selection in 82% of splits; when it does, it beats the best manual prompt on the other half in 70% of them
